We are delighted to announce that EAGLE researchers [Syed Juned Ali](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/people/syed-juned-ali), [Zhuoxun Zheng](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/people/zhuoxun-zheng), and [Dominik Bork](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/people/dominik-bork) received a **Best Paper Award at CAiSE 2026** for their paper *“Shortcut or Understanding? Diagnosing LLM Type Prediction in Conceptual Models.”*

![Syed Juned Ali receiving the Best Paper Award at CAiSE 2026](/assets/caise_best_paper_20260611.jpeg)

The paper investigates how intelligent modeling assistants predict element types in conceptual models. Using enterprise architecture and OntoUML models, the authors examine how natural-language labels, structural context, and different AI approaches influence prediction quality. Their results show that meaningful labels are the strongest signal, while structural information provides valuable support when labels are incomplete or unavailable. The study also highlights the potential of hybrid approaches that combine structurally trained models with the semantic reasoning capabilities of large language models.

This work is closely connected to EAGLE’s goal of making enterprise architecture knowledge easier to learn from and explore. Reliable interpretation of model elements is an important step toward transforming heterogeneous enterprise architecture models into structured knowledge graphs and building trustworthy AI-supported tools on top of them. The findings therefore provide valuable guidance for the intelligent model processing methods developed within EAGLE.

The award was presented at the 38th International Conference on Advanced Information Systems Engineering (CAiSE), a leading forum for research in information systems and enterprise engineering. Congratulations to Syed, Zhuoxun, and Dominik on this outstanding achievement!
