**What stops working when a server moves — and how quickly can an organization give a reliable answer?** This question opened the EAGLE presentation at TU Wien’s 12th **Blickpunkt Forschung**, “KI im Unternehmen – Forschung trifft Praxis,” on **October 15, 2026**.

On behalf of the entire project team, **Dominik Bork** presented EAGLE, our project funded by the **Austrian Research Promotion Agency (FFG)** in collaboration with **Dirigenta IT Solutions**. The presentation explored how knowledge graphs and AI can support Enterprise Architecture Management and help organizations make informed decisions about changes to their IT landscape.

![Presentation of EAGLE’s approach to connecting architecture knowledge at TU Wien’s Blickpunkt Forschung](/assets/eagle_demo_1.jpeg)

Architecture knowledge is often scattered across inventories, models, documents, and people’s heads. EAGLE is working to connect these sources in an **Enterprise Architecture Knowledge Graph** that supports:

- **Understanding dependencies and change impacts:** identifying which systems and services may be affected when part of the architecture changes.
- **Planning migration waves:** taking dependencies and operational constraints into account when organizing migrations.
- **Asking questions in natural language:** making architecture knowledge easier to explore while tracing answers back to their sources.

A central principle of EAGLE is **“AI proposes; experts decide.”** Structured knowledge, source traceability, and human judgement belong together. Our goal is to support experts with reliable information while keeping them in control of architectural decisions.
